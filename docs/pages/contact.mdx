## Legal, privacy, and publisher requests

Email [soumitsrah@cafecito.tech](mailto:soumitsrah@cafecito.tech) and use one of these subjects:

- **Privacy Request**
- **Copyright Notice**
- **Publisher Opt-Out**
- **Correction Request**
- **Publisher Licensing**
- **Abuse Report**
- **Security Report**
