# Publisher Requests and Licensing

**Status:** Draft for counsel and factual review; not approved for production reliance.

**Effective Date:** To be set on approval and publication.

Publishers, authors, rights holders, and people identified in publisher material may use this process to request a correction, source restriction, opt-out, removal, or licensing discussion.

This process is separate from a formal copyright notice. For a DMCA notice or counter-notice, use the [Copyright and Rights Complaints Policy](/company/copyright-policy).

## Submit a request

Email [soumitsrah@cafecito.tech](mailto:soumitsrah@cafecito.tech) with one of these subjects:

- **Publisher Opt-Out**
- **Content Rights Request**
- **Correction Request**
- **Publisher Licensing**

Include:

- your name, role, organization, and contact information;
- the domain, feed, canonical URLs, and Cafecito Article IDs involved;
- the action requested: stop collection, suppress full content, remove a record, correct metadata, update attribution, or discuss a license;
- the basis for your authority or connection to the content;
- the specific rights, terms, or factual issue involved; and
- any urgency, safety concern, retraction, court order, or supporting documentation.

Do not send passwords, private keys, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## Review and action

Cafecito may verify identity or authority and may temporarily restrict content while a request is reviewed. Depending on the request, we may:

- stop or limit future collection from a URL, feed, path, or domain;
- suppress content while retaining a source link and factual metadata;
- update source, author, date, or attribution fields;
- mark, refresh, or remove corrected or retracted material;
- add a source-level restriction;
- preserve information needed for legal claims, security, or audit purposes; or
- decline a request when Cafecito has a lawful basis to retain or provide the material.

We will consider the nature of the content, source status, public interest, applicable law, license terms, accuracy, and safety. If we need more information, we will contact the requestor using the information provided.

## Publisher licensing

A full-content license must expressly identify the permitted uses. Relevant rights may include access, reproduction, extraction, format conversion, caching, storage, indexing, summarization, automated analysis, quotation, API distribution, customer display, archival use, and training. These rights are separate; permission to crawl or read a feed does not necessarily include permission to redistribute full text.

Publishers should also identify:

- included and excluded properties, feeds, paths, and content types;
- syndicated, wire-service, freelance, image, video, chart, and other third-party exclusions;
- required attribution and brand rules;
- territories, languages, products, and customer channels;
- retention, correction, withdrawal, and post-termination deletion rules;
- downstream customer rights and restrictions; and
- fees, reporting, audits, term, and termination.

Licensing inquiries should use the subject **Publisher Licensing**.
