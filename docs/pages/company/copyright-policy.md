# Copyright and Rights Complaints Policy

**Status:** Draft for counsel and factual review; not approved for production reliance.

**Effective Date:** To be set on approval and publication.

Cafecito respects intellectual-property rights. This policy explains how to report content available through the Services, including a formal notice under the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (**DMCA**) when applicable.

Publication of this policy does not by itself establish Cafecito's eligibility for a DMCA safe harbor. Safe-harbor eligibility depends on the activity and compliance with all statutory requirements.

## Copyright notice

A notice should include:

1. a physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or an authorized agent;
2. identification of the copyrighted work, or a representative list if multiple works are involved;
3. identification of the material or activity claimed to be infringing and information reasonably sufficient for us to locate it, including each Cafecito Article ID, source URL, API route, and response where possible;
4. your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address;
5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the complained-of use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or law; and
6. a statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

Knowingly making a material misrepresentation may create liability. A general publisher opt-out or correction request may instead use [Publisher Requests and Licensing](/company/publisher-requests).

## Our response

We may request clarification, preserve relevant records, temporarily restrict full content or affected output, remove or disable access, stop collecting a URL or domain, notify an affected customer or source, or take other appropriate action. We may forward the notice and related contact information to the affected party or as legally required.

## Counter-notice

If material you provided was removed or disabled because of mistake or misidentification, a counter-notice should include:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of the material removed or disabled and its prior location;
3. a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the action resulted from mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address, and telephone number;
5. your consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate U.S. federal district court as specified by 17 U.S.C. § 512(g); and
6. a statement that you will accept service of process from the original notice sender or its agent.

When the statutory counter-notice process applies, Cafecito may restore material after the legally prescribed period unless the original sender timely informs us that it filed an action seeking a court order.

## Repeat infringement and standard technical measures

Repeat infringement is prohibited. Cafecito may terminate accounts of repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances and may act on a single serious violation.

Before relying on any 17 U.S.C. § 512 safe harbor, Cafecito must adopt, inform account holders of, and reasonably implement a repeat-infringer termination policy in appropriate circumstances; accommodate and not interfere with standard technical measures as required by § 512(i); register and timely renew a designated agent; publish the exact registered contact details, including a telephone number; and operate the applicable notice, user-notification, counter-notice, and restoration procedures. Publishing this draft does not satisfy those operational requirements.
