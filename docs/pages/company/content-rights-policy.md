# Third-Party Content and Attribution Policy

**Status:** Draft for counsel and factual review; not approved for production reliance.

**Effective Date:** To be set on approval and publication.

Beans helps users discover and analyze news, blogs, reports, statements, research, and other publisher material. This policy explains the boundary between Cafecito output, third-party rights, and customer use.

## What Beans may return

Depending on the route, source, and request, an Article may include:

- a canonical source URL;
- title, byline, publication time, language, image URL, and source metadata;
- Cafecito-generated or normalized categories, regions, entities, sentiments, tags, rankings, and related-coverage information;
- a publisher-supplied summary or excerpt; and
- content when it is available and the customer requests it.

Cafecito does not promise that content is complete, current, or available for every Article.

## Scraped and feed-delivered content

Cafecito may process and retain text from public pages to operate search, classification, and enrichment. Article bodies obtained solely by scraping ordinary pages are restricted from full-content output through Beans. Storage and processing do not create or expand any right to redistribute the source material. Content obtained through a publisher or source feed, official public record, platform self-post, or another non-page-scrape ingestion path may be available through the API. Availability does not itself establish customer redistribution rights.

An RSS or Atom feed is a delivery method, not an automatic grant of unrestricted copyright, republication, sublicensing, training, or archival rights. A feed may carry its own terms, license, attribution requirements, or third-party material. Customers must not infer a broad reuse license from the presence of content in a feed or API response.

## Ownership and customer rights

Publishers, authors, licensors, and other rights holders retain rights in Third-Party Content. Cafecito's software license does not apply to API data or Third-Party Content.

Unless a written agreement or machine-readable rights metadata expressly says otherwise:

- source URLs and available attribution must be preserved;
- complete content may be used only for temporary internal evaluation, search, or analysis;
- public display, redistribution, resale, syndication, bulk archiving, dataset creation, and model training are not granted;
- image URLs identify source media but do not license copying or display of the underlying image; and
- customers must obtain permissions required for their intended use.

Attribution supports transparency but does not cure an otherwise unauthorized use.

## Source fidelity

Customers should preserve:

- canonical URL;
- publisher or source name;
- author when available;
- publication date;
- any copyright or attribution notice; and
- any machine-readable license, retention, display, or deletion restriction.

Do not fabricate quotations or convert paraphrases into quotations. Preserve words such as “alleged,” “reported,” “charged,” or “according to” when they are material to meaning. Verify important information at the source.

## Corrections, withdrawals, and restrictions

A source may update, retract, or remove material. Cafecito may refresh, annotate, restrict, or remove affected records and may require customers to stop using or delete content when rights or license terms require it. See the [Corrections Policy](/company/corrections-policy) and [Publisher Requests and Licensing](/company/publisher-requests).

## No endorsement

Source identification does not imply that a publisher endorses Cafecito or a customer. Publisher trademarks and logos remain subject to their owners' rights.
