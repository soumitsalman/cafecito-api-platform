# Privacy Policy

**Status:** Draft for counsel and factual review; not approved for production reliance.

**Effective Date:** To be set on approval and publication.

This Privacy Policy explains how **Strategic Implementation Advising, LLC**, doing business through Project Cafecito (**Cafecito**, **we**, **us**, or **our**), collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information when you use Cafecito websites, the developer portal, accounts, APIs, MCP endpoints, support channels, and related services (collectively, the **Services**).

This policy distinguishes account and Service-usage information from personal information that may appear in third-party published material made searchable through Beans.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you provide

- **Account information:** name, email address, organization information, authentication identifiers, and profile information.
- **Subscription and transaction information:** plan, subscription status, billing contact, transaction records, and payment status. Payment providers process complete payment-card details; Cafecito does not need to store complete card numbers.
- **API-key information:** key names or descriptions, creation and revocation records, and account associations. Secret credentials should not be sent to support.
- **Requests and communications:** API queries, submitted URLs, request parameters, prompts or instructions sent through supported interfaces, support messages, rights requests, and feedback.

### Information collected automatically

- **Usage and device information:** IP address, user agent, timestamps, endpoints and tools used, response status, usage volume, authentication and account identifiers, and diagnostic or security events.
- **Cookies and local storage:** authentication, security, preference, and essential portal-function data. We do not use this policy to authorize cross-context behavioral advertising.

### Information from other sources

- **Account, authentication, and billing providers:** identity, profile, subscription, and transaction-status information needed to provide the Services.
- **Public and licensed sources:** publisher pages, feeds, public records, source metadata, and other published material. This material may contain names, professional information, quotations, allegations, images, or other information about identifiable people.
- **Security and fraud sources:** information used to protect accounts, investigate abuse, and comply with law.

## 2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- create and administer accounts, subscriptions, and API credentials;
- authenticate requests, meter usage, enforce limits, and provide requested output;
- operate, maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Services;
- respond to support, privacy, publisher, copyright, correction, and legal requests;
- detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our policies;
- analyze aggregate usage and product performance;
- communicate transactional, security, policy, and product updates;
- comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims; and
- index, classify, summarize, link, and provide source information and other Service output from third-party published material.

Where law requires consent for a particular use, we will seek it. Where Cafecito acts as a processor or service provider for a customer, we process covered customer personal data under the customer's instructions and any applicable data processing addendum.

## 3. How we disclose information

We may disclose personal information to:

- **service providers and processors** that provide authentication, hosting, communications, analytics, billing, security, support, and other operational services under contractual restrictions;
- **customers and users** when information is part of third-party published material or Service output requested through the APIs;
- **professional advisers** such as lawyers, accountants, auditors, and insurers;
- **authorities or other parties** when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or respond to valid legal process; and
- **transaction counterparties** in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar corporate transaction, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not sell account information or Service-usage information for money, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Beans provides third-party published material and derived metadata as part of the Services. Because privacy-law definitions vary, we will evaluate and provide any legally required notice, opt-out, or registration associated with that activity.

## 4. Published third-party material

Beans is designed to help users discover and analyze publisher material. Publicly available information may still be personal information under some laws. Cafecito may make available source URLs, titles, dates, bylines, summaries, classifications, sentiment labels, and other attributes. Available article or document content may also be provided when requested.

If you are identified in publisher material and want to request correction, restriction, or removal, contact us using the process in [Publisher Requests and Licensing](/company/publisher-requests) or the [Corrections Policy](/company/corrections-policy). A request does not necessarily require removal of accurate, lawfully processed public-interest material, but we will evaluate applicable law, source status, accuracy, safety, and rights.

## 5. Retention

We retain information only for periods reasonably necessary for the purposes described here:

- account and subscription records are retained while the account is active and afterward as needed for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, and dispute purposes;
- API and security logs are retained for operations, metering, abuse prevention, incident investigation, and legal compliance, with longer retention only when needed for a documented reason;
- support, rights, correction, and legal-request records are retained as needed to resolve the request, maintain an audit trail, and establish or defend legal claims;
- third-party published material and derived attributes are retained while needed to provide the Services, subject to source restrictions, correction or removal requests, license terms, and legal holds; and
- backups are deleted or overwritten on their normal cycle unless preservation is legally required.

We may retain information in deidentified or aggregated form when it is not reasonably capable of being linked to an individual. Cafecito will maintain an internal retention schedule and review it as the Services and legal requirements change.

## 6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your credentials and promptly reporting suspected compromise.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live and subject to legal exceptions, you may have rights to:

- know or access personal information;
- correct inaccurate personal information;
- delete personal information;
- obtain a portable copy;
- opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or certain profiling where applicable;
- limit certain uses of sensitive personal information;
- object to or restrict certain processing; and
- appeal a denied request.

To submit a request, email [soumitsrah@cafecito.tech](mailto:soumitsrah@cafecito.tech) with the subject **Privacy Request**. Describe the right you want to exercise and the account, URL, or record involved. We may verify your identity and authority. Authorized agents may submit requests where permitted by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right. To appeal a denied request where an appeal right applies, reply to the decision or email the same address with the subject **Privacy Appeal**, identify the prior request, and explain why you believe the decision should change.

Before this draft becomes effective, Cafecito will determine whether browser-based opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control apply to its activities and will implement and honor them where required.

## 8. California disclosures

If the California Consumer Privacy Act applies, California residents may exercise the rights described above. In the preceding 12 months, the categories of personal information collected and disclosed for business purposes may include identifiers; customer records; commercial and subscription information; internet or network activity; approximate location derived from IP address; professional information supplied in accounts or appearing in published material; and inferences such as classifications or sentiment labels.

The sources, purposes, and recipient categories are described in Sections 1 through 3. Cafecito does not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit under California law without providing that right. Cafecito does not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

## 9. International transfers

Cafecito is based in the United States. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where service providers operate. Where required, we use contractual or other lawful safeguards for international transfers.

## 10. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and Cafecito does not knowingly collect account information from children under 13. Accounts and API keys are available only to people who are at least 18 or the age of legal majority where they live. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information in violation of this policy.

## 11. Third-party sites and services

The Services link to publisher sites and use independent service providers. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. A source link does not mean Cafecito controls or endorses the source's privacy practices.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services or law changes. We will post the revised policy and update the effective date. We will provide additional notice when required for material changes. We will not rely only on a retroactive policy change to authorize a materially different use of information when consent or other notice is legally required.
