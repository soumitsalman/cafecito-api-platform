# Corrections and Retractions Policy

**Status:** Draft for counsel and factual review; not approved for production reliance.

**Effective Date:** To be set on approval and publication.

Cafecito aims to preserve source fidelity while clearly distinguishing publisher material from Cafecito-generated classifications and intelligence. Errors can arise from source changes, stale copies, incorrect attribution, automated extraction, classification, clustering, summarization, or identity ambiguity.

## What can be corrected

Correction requests may concern:

- source URL, publisher, author, title, date, language, or content type;
- categories, regions, entities, sentiments, tags, rankings, or Story grouping;
- an incomplete or stale summary or content value;
- a publisher correction, retraction, or withdrawal;
- a mistaken identity or conflation of different people or organizations;
- a fabricated or inaccurate quotation; or
- an Espresso Event, Signal, or evidence link that does not faithfully reflect its source.

## Submit a correction

Email [soumitsrah@cafecito.tech](mailto:soumitsrah@cafecito.tech) with the subject **Correction Request**. Include the Cafecito record ID, canonical source URL, the field or statement at issue, the requested correction, and supporting source material. State whether the issue involves safety, a minor, private information, a retraction, or a pending legal matter.

## How we may respond

After review, Cafecito may:

- refresh information from the source;
- correct metadata or derived labels;
- separate mistakenly merged identities or records;
- annotate a correction or retraction;
- suppress content while preserving a canonical link and minimal factual metadata;
- remove a record or source from output;
- invalidate related or cached output where practicable; or
- decline a request and explain the basis where appropriate.

For publisher-authored text, Cafecito ordinarily links to the publisher's correction rather than rewriting the publisher's work. A correction to Cafecito-generated output does not alter the underlying publisher article.

## Retractions and downstream use

When we learn that a source has retracted or materially corrected a work, we may restrict or update related output. Customers are responsible for checking source status before consequential use and for honoring correction, deletion, or withdrawal notices communicated through applicable metadata, documentation, or direct notice.

## Appeals

If you believe a request was misunderstood, reply with the subject **Correction Appeal** and provide the prior request, disputed decision, and additional evidence.
