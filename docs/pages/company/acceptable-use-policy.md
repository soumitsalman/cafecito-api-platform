# Acceptable Use Policy

**Status:** Draft for counsel and factual review; not approved for production reliance.

**Effective Date:** To be set on approval and publication.

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to all Cafecito Services and is incorporated into the [Terms of Use](/company/terms-of-use). Capitalized terms have the meanings given in the Terms.

You may not use the Services, directly or indirectly, to do any of the following.

## 1. Break the law or violate rights

- Violate applicable law, regulation, court order, sanctions, or export controls.
- Infringe copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, database, contractual, or other rights.
- Defame, harass, threaten, stalk, dox, exploit, or endanger a person.
- Collect, infer, publish, or use sensitive or personal information unlawfully.
- Remove or falsify copyright notices, source information, author credits, canonical links, rights metadata, or attribution.
- Misrepresent source material, generated output, or Cafecito's relationship with a publisher.

## 2. Circumvent access or content controls

- Bypass a paywall, login, authentication requirement, CAPTCHA, rate limit, robots restriction, publisher opt-out, or other technical or contractual control.
- Use stolen, shared, fabricated, or unauthorized credentials, cookies, tokens, or session data.
- Request or combine output in a way designed to reconstruct content Cafecito or a publisher has restricted.
- Direct Cafecito to access private, confidential, or non-public systems or material without authorization.

## 3. Misuse publisher content

Unless a written agreement or machine-readable rights metadata expressly permits the use, you may not:

- publicly republish or redistribute complete third-party articles or documents;
- build a substitute publisher site, archive, feed, searchable corpus, or competing content database;
- resell, sublicense, syndicate, bulk-download, or provide Third-Party Content as a standalone product;
- use Third-Party Content for model training, fine-tuning, dataset creation, or permanent RAG storage;
- retain content longer than an applicable license or documented retention period;
- remove required source links or present content as your own; or
- continue using content after a restriction, withdrawal, or deletion instruction applies.

## 4. Conduct harmful profiling or high-impact decisions

You may not use the Services as a consumer reporting agency or to create or furnish a consumer report. You may not use Service output as the sole or determinative basis for decisions about employment, housing, credit, insurance, education, healthcare, public benefits, legal services, or another high-impact domain.

You may not use the Services for unlawful surveillance, facial recognition, biometric identification, location tracking, sensitive-trait inference, or profiling of children or vulnerable people.

## 5. Spread deception or unsafe content

- Fabricate quotations, sources, evidence, or endorsements.
- Present allegations as established facts or knowingly distribute materially false or misleading content.
- Use automated summaries or classifications without appropriate verification in a context where an error could materially harm a person.
- Provide regulated professional advice while representing Cafecito output as verified professional guidance.
- Generate malware, facilitate credential theft, phishing, spam, fraud, or other abusive activity.

## 6. Attack or overload the Services

- Probe, scan, or test vulnerabilities without written authorization.
- Interfere with, disrupt, overload, or evade limits on the Services.
- Reverse engineer the Services except to the limited extent law expressly permits despite this restriction.
- Scrape the developer portal or APIs instead of using documented interfaces, or copy documentation for a competing service.
- Share API keys publicly, conceal the source of abusive traffic, or create accounts to evade suspension or limits.

## 7. Enforcement

Cafecito may investigate suspected violations, limit or block requests, remove or restrict content, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve relevant records, and cooperate with lawful investigations. We may act immediately when needed to address legal, security, rights, or safety risk.

Report abuse to [soumitsrah@cafecito.tech](mailto:soumitsrah@cafecito.tech) with the subject **Abuse Report** and include the relevant account, API request, URL, and supporting information.
