# Data Processing Addendum Template

**Draft for privacy counsel review — not approved for signature**

This Data Processing Addendum (**DPA**) supplements the agreement (**Agreement**) between the customer identified in an order form (**Customer**) and **Strategic Implementation Advising, LLC**, doing business through Project Cafecito (**Cafecito**).

This draft covers personal data Cafecito processes on Customer's behalf to provide the Services. It does not automatically treat Cafecito's independently collected account data or third-party published content as Customer-controlled data. The parties must document their actual roles by processing activity.

## 1. Definitions

- **Applicable Data Protection Law** means privacy, data-protection, and breach-notification law applicable to the processing.
- **Customer Personal Data** means personal data Cafecito processes on Customer's behalf under the Agreement.
- **Controller**, **processor**, **business**, **service provider**, **consumer**, **data subject**, **personal data**, **personal information**, **process**, **sell**, and **share** have the meanings in applicable law.
- **Security Incident** means unauthorized access to, acquisition, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction of Customer Personal Data, excluding unsuccessful attempts that do not compromise security.
- **Subprocessor** means a third party engaged by Cafecito to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Scope and roles

2.1 Customer determines the purposes and means of processing Customer Personal Data and acts as controller or business. Cafecito acts as processor or service provider only for the processing described in Schedule 1.

2.2 Cafecito acts independently for account administration, fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, service analytics using appropriately aggregated or deidentified data, and third-party published material it selects and processes for its own Services, unless the parties expressly agree otherwise.

2.3 Customer is responsible for lawful instructions, notices, consents, legal bases, and rights in data submitted to the Services.

## 3. Instructions and restrictions

3.1 Cafecito will process Customer Personal Data only to provide, secure, support, and improve the contracted Services; comply with documented lawful instructions; and comply with law.

3.2 Cafecito will not sell or share Customer Personal Data, retain/use/disclose it outside the direct business relationship, combine it with data received from another person except as legally permitted, or use it for targeted advertising, except as expressly authorized by Customer and applicable law.

3.3 Cafecito will inform Customer if an instruction appears to violate applicable law, unless prohibited from doing so.

## 4. Confidentiality

Cafecito will ensure personnel authorized to process Customer Personal Data are bound by confidentiality duties and receive appropriate privacy and security training.

## 5. Security

Cafecito will maintain reasonable and appropriate administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards proportionate to the nature of processing and risk. Before signature, Schedule 2 must describe actual controls, including:

- access control and least privilege;
- credential and key management;
- encryption in transit and, where appropriate, at rest;
- logging, monitoring, vulnerability management, and secure development;
- backup, restoration, availability, and disaster recovery;
- incident response;
- vendor and subprocessor diligence;
- retention and secure deletion; and
- periodic testing and risk assessment.

Do not promise certifications or control maturity that Cafecito has not verified.

## 6. Subprocessors

6.1 Customer authorizes the Subprocessors listed in Schedule 3.

6.2 Cafecito will impose data-protection obligations appropriate to the processing and remain responsible for each Subprocessor's performance to the extent required by applicable law and the Agreement.

6.3 The parties must select a notice and objection mechanism before signature: [GENERAL WRITTEN AUTHORIZATION WITH CHANGE NOTICE / SPECIFIC AUTHORIZATION].

6.4 A current Subprocessor list and change-notice method must be operational before using this DPA.

## 7. Individual rights

Taking into account the nature of processing, Cafecito will provide reasonable assistance for access, correction, deletion, portability, opt-out, restriction, objection, and appeal requests. If Cafecito receives a request concerning Customer Personal Data, it will direct the requester to Customer unless law permits or requires Cafecito to respond.

## 8. Government requests

Unless prohibited, Cafecito will notify Customer of legally binding requests for Customer Personal Data and provide reasonable information so Customer may seek protection. Cafecito will disclose only data legally required.

## 9. Security incidents

9.1 Cafecito will notify Customer without undue delay after confirming a Security Incident involving Customer Personal Data.

9.2 Notice will include available information about the nature of the incident, affected data and people, likely consequences, mitigation, and a contact. Information may be provided in phases.

9.3 Notification is not an admission of fault or liability. Customer remains responsible for notices it is legally required to provide, with Cafecito's reasonable assistance.

9.4 The parties must state any contractual notification deadline in the signed DPA and ensure operations can meet it.

## 10. Assessments and audits

Cafecito will provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance. The parties should first use current independent reports, questionnaires, and documentation. On-site audits, frequency, confidentiality, cost, scope, and remediation must be defined before signature.

## 11. Return and deletion

At Customer's choice and subject to law, Cafecito will return or delete Customer Personal Data after the Services end. The signed DPA must define active-system and backup timelines, deletion verification, legal holds, security records, and deidentified data. Deletion does not apply to independently controlled data outside this DPA.

## 12. International transfers

Before transferring Customer Personal Data from a jurisdiction that restricts transfer, the parties will use an applicable lawful mechanism. If European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Swiss data is in scope, counsel must attach the correct Standard Contractual Clauses, UK addendum, transfer-impact materials, and role/module selections.

## 13. U.S. state terms

To the extent applicable, Cafecito will act as a service provider/contractor or processor; process data only for specified business purposes and documented instructions; provide required assistance; allow appropriate compliance monitoring; notify Customer if it can no longer comply; and permit reasonable steps to stop and remediate unauthorized use.

State-specific terms must be added after counsel identifies applicable states and roles.

## 14. Conflict and liability

If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement on personal-data processing, this DPA controls. Liability under this DPA is subject to the Agreement unless the signed DPA expressly states otherwise. Counsel must approve any special cap, exclusion, or indemnity.
