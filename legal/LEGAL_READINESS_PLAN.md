# API Legal Readiness Plan

**Internal worklog — not a public policy or customer agreement.**

## Current position

All Cafecito products are currently available as free tier. Cafecito collects data from nearly 15,000 different public-information and publicly available sources/outlets daily. `cafecito.tech` is the canonical public policy source. This directory must remain outside portal navigation, generated Markdown, search, sitemaps, and `llms.txt`.

Public availability, attribution, or API delivery does not establish customer rights to display, redistribute, archive, resell, build datasets from, permanently store for retrieval-augmented generation, or train models on third-party expression.

## Current documentation boundaries

- Corporate Terms, Privacy, AUP, content rights, copyright, publisher, correction, collection, and editorial policies live only on `cafecito.tech`.
- The developer portal documents API-specific behavior, authentication, limits, parameters, response fields, and rights warnings.
- No document represents paid checkout, recurring billing, paid support, an SLA, a commercial content license, or an enterprise agreement as operational.
- Public descriptions use generic terms: public-information collection systems, enrichment systems, source catalog, content storage, and API output layer.

## Readiness gates

- [ ] Counsel reviews the actual systems, copies, retention, outputs, customer uses, source terms, and jurisdictional exposure.
- [ ] A source-specific rights register records allowed collection, processing, retention, display, API distribution, summarization, retrieval, training, media, territory, customer, and expiration scopes.
- [ ] Unknown, expired, withdrawn, or conflicting rights states fail closed for third-party expression.
- [ ] Every Beans `full_content` operation is tested against the rights register without changing unrelated route contracts.
- [ ] Publisher, copyright, privacy, correction, abuse, and security workflows are owned and tested through `support@cafecito.tech`.
- [ ] Data mapping, retention, service-provider, security, incident, and privacy-rights records reflect production.
- [ ] Marketing claims avoid complete-coverage, guaranteed-accuracy, instantaneous-delivery, source-endorsement, and unrestricted-rights implications.
- [ ] Monetization remains disabled until corporate monetization prerequisites, commercial terms, insurance, rights, and assent controls are complete.

## Unresolved full-content item

Full-content availability remains an unresolved readiness item. The current documentation warning is not a rights-enforcement control and does not grant customers downstream rights. Counsel and engineering must approve a fail-closed source-specific policy before monetization or broader distribution.
